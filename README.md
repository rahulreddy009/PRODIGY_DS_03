# PRODIGY_DS_03 — Bank Marketing Decision Tree Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is part of the **Prodigy InfoTech Data Science Internship – Task 03**.

The project analyzes the **Bank Marketing dataset** using **Tableau** to understand customer characteristics and factors associated with whether a customer subscribes to a bank product or service.

## Objective

The main objective is to visualize relationships between customer demographics, financial information, and marketing outcomes.

The target variable is:

- **`y = yes`** → Customer subscribed
- **`y = no`** → Customer did not subscribe

## Dataset

The project uses the **Bank Marketing dataset** from a bank marketing campaign.

### Important Features

- `age` — Customer age
- `job` — Type of job
- `marital` — Marital status
- `education` — Education level
- `default` — Credit default status
- `balance` — Average yearly account balance
- `housing` — Housing loan status
- `loan` — Personal loan status
- `contact` — Communication type
- `duration` — Last contact duration
- `campaign` — Number of contacts during the campaign
- `pdays` — Days since previous campaign contact
- `previous` — Number of previous contacts
- `poutcome` — Previous campaign outcome
- `y` — Final subscription outcome

## Tableau Visualizations

### 1. Customer Distribution by Job

Shows the distribution of customers across different job categories.

### 2. Purchase Outcome by Job

Compares customers who subscribed and did not subscribe across different job categories.

### 3. Housing and Loan vs Purchase

Analyzes the relationship between housing loans, personal loans, and subscription outcomes.

### 4. Age and Balance vs Purchase

A scatter plot showing the relationship between customer age, account balance, and subscription outcome.

## Dashboard

The final Tableau dashboard combines all four visualizations into a single interactive dashboard.

The dashboard provides an overview of:

- Customer job distribution
- Purchase/subscription outcomes
- Housing loan status
- Personal loan status
- Customer age
- Account balance

Interactive filters are included to explore the data by different customer characteristics.
